# Atlaspec Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Atlaspec welcomes contributors regardless of experience, background, identity,
or affiliation. We expect discussion to be technically rigorous and personally
respectful.

Positive participation includes:

- giving specific, reproducible feedback;
- distinguishing evidence from opinion;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- critiquing work without attacking people; and
- helping newcomers understand project and benchmark conventions.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, or
coordinated manipulation of project discussions or benchmark results.

## Research and evaluation conduct

Do not present post-selected results as independent evidence, inspect sealed
evaluation material to tune an implementation, or pressure contributors to
weaken a preregistered gate after results are known.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community.

For a private conduct report, open a private advisory at
<https://github.com/tauptlab/atlaspec/security/advisories/new> and prefix the
title with `[Conduct]`. Include relevant links and the minimum information
needed to investigate. Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent
possible.
