03 · PRODUCT BOUNDARY

An opinionated cartographic IR—not a general GIS.

Deterministic compilation trades arbitrary renderer freedom for a smaller, auditable, safety-oriented authoring surface.

ATLASPEC 0.2 DOES
  • Represent explicit map-reading intent and field semantics
  • Reject unsupported or misleading cartographic mappings
  • Compile deterministic MapLibre artifacts
  • Compile a fail-closed portable Vega-Lite subset
  • Record diagnostics and inferred decisions
ATLASPEC 0.2 DOES NOT
  • Perform routing, visibility, shadows, or geoprocessing
  • Replace GIS, GeoJSON, MapLibre, or Vega-Lite
  • Support arbitrary renderer-native customization
  • Guarantee map-reading effectiveness or production safety
  • Turn the current development result into a sealed holdout
