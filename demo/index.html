03 · THE PRACTICAL ARCHITECTURE

AI writes intent. Solvers produce evidence.

Atlaspec is an intermediate language for map rendering. The next layer connects spatial-analysis intent to auditable computation.

01

Natural request

“Find an entrance with less shade at 3 PM that is wheelchair accessible.”

→
02

Spatial spec

Declare the coordinate system, units, time, obstacles, constraints, and tolerances.

→
03

Deterministic solver

Compute solar position, line of sight, and graph costs with versioned algorithms.

→
04

Evidence + Atlaspec

Preserve result polygons, routes, assumptions, and checksums in maps and reports.

2D

Coordinates + attributes

Property analysis, catchments, containment, distance, and network routing

2.5D

footprint + height

Solar access, shadows, CCTV visibility, viewsheds, and simple signal coverage

3D

Surfaces + meshes + floors

Eaves, balconies, interiors, complex roofs, and volumetric obstacles
