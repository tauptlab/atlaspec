# Media assets

`atlaspec-60s-demo.mp4`, its poster, and subtitle track are generated from
`scripts/render_launch_video.py`. The video is designed for silent autoplay and
uses only project-owned graphics and text.

These assets may be redistributed under the repository's MIT License. Benchmark
numbers shown in the video retain the evidence boundaries documented in
[`docs/LAUNCH_KIT.md`](../docs/LAUNCH_KIT.md).
